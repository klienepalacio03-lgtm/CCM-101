# Mission Reflection

Completing this cloud computing mission helped me understand how cloud platforms can be used to solve real-world business and organizational problems. Among AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud Platform (GCP), **Microsoft Azure impressed me the most** because of its wide range of services, scalability, security features, and integration with other Microsoft technologies. I also found Azure suitable for a university environment because it can support applications, databases, storage, user access, and backup requirements.

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities. All three provide cloud services for **computing, storage, networking, databases, security, and identity management**. They also support scalability, allowing organizations to increase or decrease resources depending on demand. Another similarity is that they offer flexible pricing models and tools that help organizations monitor and manage their cloud resources.

Business requirements strongly influence the selection of a cloud platform. Organizations need to consider their budget, security requirements, expected number of users, performance, scalability, existing technologies, and technical skills of their employees. The best cloud provider is not always the one with the most features, but the one that can meet the organization's specific needs effectively and efficiently.

Acting as a **Cloud Solutions Consultant** taught me how important it is to understand the problem before choosing a technology. I learned how to compare cloud providers, identify appropriate cloud services, consider scalability and security, and explain why a particular solution is suitable for a business or organization.

After completing this mission, my **GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio** has improved significantly. I added documentation, cloud provider comparisons, infrastructure information, Linux command results, screenshots, and reflections. The portfolio now shows not only what I learned but also how I applied my knowledge through practical cloud computing activities. Overall, this mission improved my confidence in analyzing cloud solutions and making technology recommendations.
